Det 088 Suspense Sync

A Chrome extension for AFROTC Detachment 088 cadets.

Det 088 Suspense Sync copies the suspenses you see on the Dragon Portal (det088.org) into your Google Calendar and keeps them up to date. When a suspense is added, moved, or removed on the portal, your calendar follows.

What it does

  • Creates a "Det 088 - My Suspenses" calendar with the suspenses assigned to you, including any extensions you've been granted.
  • Optionally creates a "Det 088 - Flight Suspenses" calendar with every suspense for your flight.
  • Marks suspenses you've completed as done and turns off their reminders.
  • Checks for changes on a schedule you choose and whenever you open the portal.

What it can access

The extension can only see and change the calendars it creates. It cannot read or edit your other calendars, email, or anything else in your Google account. It does not send your data to any server other than Google Calendar.

Installing

Get the extension folder from your flight leadership, then load it in Chrome at chrome://extensions with Developer mode turned on. Log in to det088.org, open the extension, and click "Connect Google & sync now".
