Privacy Policy

Det 088 Suspense Sync · Effective October 26, 2026

Det 088 Suspense Sync ("the extension") is a Chrome extension that copies suspenses from the Dragon Portal (det088.org) into the user's Google Calendar. This policy explains what data the extension uses and where it goes.

Data the extension reads

  • Dragon Portal data. Using the login session you already have in your browser, the extension reads your user ID, your flight number, the current semester, and the suspenses for your flight and squadron (titles, descriptions, due dates, categories, who assigned them, extensions, and completion status).
  • Google Calendar. The extension requests the calendar.app.created permission, which only lets it see and manage calendars it creates. It cannot read or change any of your other calendars or events.

How the data is used

Suspense information is used only to create, update, and delete events on the "Det 088" calendars in your own Google account. Nothing else is done with it.

Where the data is stored

  • Events are stored in your Google Calendar, under your control. You can delete them, or the whole calendar, at any time.
  • The extension keeps a small record in your browser's local extension storage: the IDs of the calendars and events it created, your settings, and the result of the last sync. This never leaves your computer.
  • The extension has no server of its own. Your data is not sent to the developer or any third party. Data travels only between your browser, det088.org, and Google.

Sharing

The extension does not sell, share, or transfer your data, and does not use it for advertising, analytics, or any purpose other than syncing your calendar.

Google API Services

The extension's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data

Uninstalling the extension deletes its local storage. To remove the events, delete the "Det 088" calendars in Google Calendar. To revoke the extension's Google access, visit myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
